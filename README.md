## 🎯 The Mission

<details>
<summary><b>🧬 From Pre-Med to Cybersecurity — My Career Change Story</b></summary>

My journey into technology wasn't exactly traditional.

In 2020, I began exploring computers and developing an understanding of how technology works. Before cybersecurity, my academic and career interests were centered around understanding the human body and its functions through a pre-med path.

Eventually, my curiosity shifted toward computers, systems, and cybersecurity.

Interestingly, I discovered that the two fields aren't as different as they might seem.

Think about it:

- 🫀 The human body requires continuous monitoring.
- 💻 Computer systems require continuous monitoring.
- 🧬 Doctors investigate symptoms to identify underlying conditions.
- 🔍 Security professionals investigate indicators to identify potential threats.
- 🩺 Preventative care helps maintain a healthy body.
- 🛡️ Proactive security helps maintain a healthy digital environment.

**Different systems. Similar principles.**

My objective is to help organizations maintain a healthy, secure environment by identifying weaknesses, detecting threats, and improving defensive capabilities.

The tables have turned, but the investigative mindset remains the same.
